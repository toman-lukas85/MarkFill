MarkFill

Write, fill in, and export Markdown documents.

For Mac and iPad. Your documents stay on your device.

Why MarkFill

I kept needing a simple way to create, edit, and read Markdown files, so I built one. A few extras came from everyday work: filling in templates, bringing in Word documents, and exporting the result.

It’s here to make your work easier.

On devices with Apple Intelligence, the Assistant can also draft documents and diagrams. It runs on your device, so your documents stay with you.

Lukas Toman

Writing and formatting

Text styles

Bold, italic, strikethrough, inline code, and highlight, from the formatting bar, the Format menu, or the keyboard.

Structure

Headings, bulleted and numbered lists, checklists, and quotes. Headings also build an outline you can jump through.

Links, tables, and diagrams

Insert links and tables in a click, and write Mermaid diagrams that appear as drawings, even without an internet connection.

Diagram Editor

Build a flowchart from cards: steps, branches, connections, and jumps, without writing Mermaid. Click a diagram in the preview to change it.

Images

Paste or drag images in. They are stored inside the document, so a .md file stays complete when you share it.

Component Library

Ready-made blocks such as form fields, signature tables, checklists, and diagrams. Drag them into a document.

Find and replace

Press Command-F to find text, then replace one match at a time or all of them.

Four modes

Edit, Split, Fill, and Preview. See your text next to the finished document as you type.

Templates and forms

25 templates

Architecture, quality assurance, management, operations, process automation, data models, and UI/UX design.

Template fields

Write {{Customer Name}} anywhere. Fields become a form, and a value you enter updates every copy, in diagrams too.

Paper forms

Turn dotted lines and empty signature rows into fields you can fill in.

Checklists

Task lists become checkboxes you can tick off, one by one or all at once. Boxes from Word, such as ☐ and ☒, work too.

Import and export

Word import

Turn a .docx file into Markdown, with its headings, lists, tables, links, and images. Your Word file stays unchanged.

Export

Save as PDF, Word, or Markdown, or copy as HTML. A completed copy keeps your template unchanged.

Assistant

Documents

Describe what you need. Details you leave out become fields you can fill in later.

Diagrams

List the steps, one per line, and get a flowchart. Edit the steps and create it again to change it.

Fields

Finds names, dates, amounts, and contacts in a document in an instant. You choose which ones become fields.

For everyone

Light and dark

Follow your system or choose Light or Dark yourself.

Keyboard

Every command is in the menu bar, and common commands have shortcuts.

VoiceOver

Every control has a name and a description.
