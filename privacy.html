MarkFill Support Privacy Terms

Privacy

Last updated November 6, 2026

MarkFill does not collect your data. There is no account, no analytics, and no advertising.

Your documents

Your documents stay in the files and folders you choose. MarkFill keeps a list of recent documents on your device so you can reopen them.

Assistant

The Assistant uses the language model built into your device. Your documents and requests are not sent to MarkFill or to any other service.

Internet connections

MarkFill works without an account, but it connects to the internet in these cases:

  • Diagrams. When a document with a Mermaid diagram is shown, the app downloads the Mermaid library from jsDelivr. jsDelivr receives your IP address, as with any web request. Your document is not sent.
  • Images from the web. If a document refers to an image by web address, that image is downloaded from its website.
  • Links. Links you open go to your web browser.

Children

MarkFill does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page.

Contact

Questions are welcome on the Support page.
