Release Notes

Version 1.0.1 for Mac

  • Click a heading in the outline and the preview follows. This works in Split, Fill, and Preview, and the heading is briefly highlighted.
  • The outline now takes you to the right heading, even when several headings share the same name.
  • Headings with bold or other formatting can now be reached from the outline.
  • Selecting a heading works while you are searching the outline.
  • The Return to Editor button now matches the other buttons.

Version 1.0.1 for iPad

  • Tap a heading in the outline and the preview follows. This works in Split, Fill, and Preview, and the heading is briefly highlighted.
  • The editor now scrolls to the selected heading and keeps it above the keyboard.
  • Headings with bold or other formatting can now be reached from the outline.
  • A hint suggests landscape when you hold your iPad upright. It disappears when you rotate, and you can close it.
