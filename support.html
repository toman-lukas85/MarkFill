Support

To report a problem or ask a question, open an issue on GitHub. Please include your device, system version, and the steps that led to the problem.

Which devices are supported?

MarkFill needs macOS 27.2 or iPadOS 27.2 or later.

Why is the Assistant unavailable?

The Assistant needs a device that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on. Write your requests in English. Finding fields works on every device.

Which Word files can I import?

MarkFill imports .docx files. Older .doc files and password-protected documents must first be saved as unprotected .docx files in Word.

What is not imported from Word?

Headers, footers, footnotes, comments, charts, and SmartArt are not imported. The import dialog tells you when something is left out.

Why don’t my diagrams appear?

Check the diagram’s text for a typing mistake. Diagrams are drawn inside the app, so they appear without an internet connection.

Why can’t a diagram open in the Diagram Editor?

The Diagram Editor opens flowcharts. Diagrams with groups, styles, dotted or thick lines, or other diagram kinds stay editable in the text.

Where are my images stored?

Images are stored inside the document, so a .md file stays complete when you move or share it.
